ABOUT THE STUDIO

Designed for the way islands live.

NEXA ARCH STUDIO is a remote-first design studio based in Bangladesh, serving clients in the Maldives. We develop inspiring residential and hospitality concepts with practical spatial thinking and clear visual storytelling.

Our focus is concept design, interiors and visualization. Technical construction documents, statutory submissions and certifications require appropriately qualified and locally authorized professionals.
